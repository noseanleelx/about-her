To The Sweetest Person...

Abigale Low Shin Yi

I wanted to create a special little corner of the internet just to remind you of how much you mean to me. Every single picture on this string holds one of my absolute favorite picture of you or us.

I don't even know where to begin because there are honestly so many things I want to say to you. I could probably write pages and pages about you, about us, and about everything you've done for me, and I still don't think I would be able to properly explain how grateful I am that you came into my life.

Thank you for bringing so much warmth, laughter, and love into my life every single day. I'm so lucky to have you by my side. Even though now we not really are together anymore, I just want to tell you how much i love you. Thank you for being there with me when i needed you. Thank you for all the little things you've done that you probably didn't even realize meant so much to me. Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every moment we spent together, every time you listened to me, every time you made me smile when I wasn't expecting to, and every memory that you've given me. I notice the way you talk about things you're excited about. I notice the way you react when something makes you happy. I notice your little habits, your expressions, the way you laugh, the way you can make an ordinary moment feel special without even trying.

You are always there when i needed you, at my lowest and when i need someone to cry with me and laugh with me. I'm grateful that I got to meet you. I'm grateful that I got to know you. I'm grateful that I got to experience what it feels like to have someone who means this much to me. I'm grateful for every moment, even the ordinary ones. You've taught me a lot too. You've taught me that caring about someone isn't just about saying "I love you" or giving someone gifts or saying nice things. It's about listening. It's about understanding. It's about being patient. It's about trying. It's about learning from your mistakes. It's about appreciating someone while they're beside you instead of only realizing their value afterward. And I know I'm not perfect.

I know there have been times when I made mistakes. There have been times when I didn't handle things the way I should have. There have been times when I may have hurt you, disappointed you, confused you, or made you feel like I didn't appreciate you enough. For those moments, I'm genuinely sorry. I don't want this letter to pretend that everything has always been perfect, because it hasn't. But I also don't want my mistakes to erase all the things I'm grateful for.My mistakes make me realise how important it is to appreciate someone while you have the chance. I don't want to take you for granted. I don't want to become so comfortable with having you in my life that I forget how special it is that you're there. I don't want to assume you'll always be there. I want to appreciate every moment I get with you.

I love you abigale. I love you to the moon and back and i always will/
